What are our Treasured Places?

Our Treasured Places is a collection of protected public lands in the United States. The list includes:

433National
Park
units
133National
Monuments
17National
Conservation
Areas
496 Treasured Places after overlap

That’s every national park, monument, and conservation area in the country—special places managed by the National Park Service, US Forest Service, Bureau of Land Management, and others. After accounting for overlap, there are a combined total of 496 Treasured Places on the list.

That’s 496 of our nation’s best public lands.

These places protect some of our most iconic and inspiring landscapes, and also interpret some of our most important historical lessons. Simply put, these places tell the story of America—its lands, and its people.
