Abbreviations guide

Designation codes used in the Type column above.

  • NB — National Battlefield
  • NBP — National Battlefield Park
  • NBS — National Battlefield Site
  • NCA — National Conservation Area
  • EHP — Ecological and Historic Preserve
  • IHS — International Historic Site
  • NHP — National Historical Park
  • NHP&EP — National Historical Park and Ecological Preserve
  • NHP&P — National Historical Park and Preserve
  • NHRES — National Historical Reserve
  • NHS — National Historic Site
  • NL — National Lakeshore
  • NM — National Monument
  • NM&HisShr — National Monument and Historic Shrine
  • NMEM — National Memorial
  • NMP — National Military Park
  • NP — National Park
  • NPRES — National Preserve
  • NR — National River
  • NRA — National Recreation Area
  • NRES — National Reserve
  • NRR — National Recreational River
  • NRRA — National River and Recreation Area
  • NS — National Seashore
  • NSR — National Scenic River
  • NSRW — National Scenic Riverway
  • NST — National Scenic Trail
  • NVM — National Volcanic Monument
  • P — Park
  • PKWY — Parkway
  • S&RR — Scenic and Recreational River
  • WR — Wild River
  • WSR — Wild and Scenic River
  • – — Other NPS-administered site
