Updates

Changes to the list, newest first.

4 new additions in Jan 2025

In the waning days of his term, Biden designated four additional national monuments:

  • Carlisle Federal Indian Boarding School in Pennsylvania (NPS)
  • Chuckwalla in California (BLM)
  • Frances Perkins in Maine (NPS)
  • Sáttítla Highlands in California (USFS)

3 new NPS additions in Nov 2023

On November 16, 2023, three existing national scenic trails—Ice Age NST, New England NST, and North Country NST—were administratively redesignated as official National Park units. All three were already administered by the National Park Service.

They have been added to the Treasured Places list.
